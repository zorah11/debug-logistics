02 — HOW WE WORK

We do not spray first and ask questions later.

The property tells us where to begin. We inspect the affected space, identify the conditions helping the pest survive, and agree on a treatment plan before work starts.

  1. 01
    Inspect the site

    We look for activity, entry points, nesting areas and safety considerations.

  2. 02
    Explain the method

    You receive preparation guidance and a clear account of the treatment.

  3. 03
    Treat and document

    Our technicians use appropriate PPE and record each service visit.

  4. 04
    Reduce the return risk

    We advise on exclusion, hygiene and monitoring specific to your property.
