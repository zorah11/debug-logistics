About Debug Services

Built on skill.
Led by integrity.

Debug Services is the pest-management trade name of Debug Logistics Inc., incorporated in 2021 and shaped by more than a decade of field experience.

Our story

A more thoughtful standard of pest control.

Our founders saw an industry too often defined by rushed visits, excessive chemicals and poor communication. They created a company where technical results and human-centred service belong together.

Today, our work is guided by safety, rapid response, professional conduct and respect for every client. We are a woman-owned business, co-owned alongside a Person with Disabilities director, and committed to an inclusive workplace.
