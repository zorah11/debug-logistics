About Debug Logistics

Professional supply support, built on clarity.

Debug Logistics Ltd is a Kampala-based Ugandan company registered under General Supplies. We work with customers to understand their needs and coordinate suitable supply arrangements.

Who we are

A responsive partner for general supply needs.

We believe dependable service starts with careful listening and clear communication.

Our approach is practical: understand the request, confirm the relevant details, prepare a quotation and coordinate the agreed supply. We do not assume that every item is immediately available, which is why confirmation is an important part of every enquiry.
