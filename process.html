How we work

A clear path from request to supply.

Our process is designed to reduce uncertainty. Each step confirms the information needed before the next one begins.

The process

Four practical steps.

Good supply arrangements begin with good information and straightforward communication.

Customer sends an enquiry

Contact us by form, phone, email or WhatsApp. Share the item description, estimated quantity, timeline and delivery location where possible.

Requirements are confirmed

We review the request, ask any necessary questions and confirm the relevant specifications before preparing commercial details.

A quotation is prepared

We prepare a quotation based on the confirmed request and available supply options for your review.

Supply and delivery are arranged

After acceptance, the agreed supply and delivery details are coordinated and communicated with the customer.

Before you enquire

Useful details make the process faster.

You do not need a perfect brief, but a few specifics help us assess your request.

  • What you need supplied
  • Specifications, sizes or standards
  • Approximate quantities
  • Preferred delivery date and location
